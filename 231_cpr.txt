PARAMEDIC
Clinical Indications:
Basic life support for the patient in cardiac arrest.
Procedure
 
1.
 Assess the patient's responsiveness (no breathing or no normal breathing).
 
2.
 Activate Emergency Response/Get Defibrillator.
 
3.
 Start CPR -->Push Hard and Fast-->adequate rate and depth with complete chest recoil after each compression, MINIMIZE
Interruptions in compressions, AVOID Excessive ventilation.
 
4.
 C-A-B (Not ABC): Compressions--Airway--Breathing.

7.

8.
 Document the time and procedure in the Patient Care Report (PCR).
