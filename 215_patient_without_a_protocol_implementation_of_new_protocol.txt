Procedure for Patient Without a Protocol
1
. 
Treatment and medical direction for all patient encounters, which can be triaged into an EMS patient protocol, is to be initiated by protocol.
2
. 
When confronted with an emergency or situation that does not fit into an existing EMS patient care protocol, the 
Universal Patient Care
Protocol should be used to treat the patient, and a Medical Control Physician should be contacted for further instructions.
Implementation of New Protocols, Procedures, Equipment or Medications
1
. 
From time to time, based on available medical literature, evidence based medicine and best practices, it will be necessary to implement a new
protocol, procedure, piece of equipment or medication after distribution of the most current version of the protocol.
2
. 
Due to constraints of logistics, supply, distribution and education, it may be necessary for the EMT, Paramedic to utilize the older version of the
protocol until such time as sufficient supplies are available and training has been completed.
