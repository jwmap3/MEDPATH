Adverse Reactions
Most common adverse reactions ( > 5% ) include transient chromaturia (abnormal coloration of urine), erythema, rash, increased blood pressure,
nausea, headache, and injection site reactions.
Precautions
Known anaphylactic reactions to hydroxocobalamin or cyanocobalamin
