Clinical Indications:
Obstruction of the airway (secondary to secretions, blood, or any other substance) in a patient currently being assessed by an airway adjunct
such as a naso-tracheal tube, endotracheal tube, tracheotomy tube, or a cricothyrotomy tube.
Procedure
 
1. 
Ensure suction device is in proper working order.
 
2.
 Preoxygenate the patient with 100% Non-rebreather mask.
 
3.
 Attach suction catheter to suction device, keeping sterile plastic covering over catheter.
 
4.
 For all devices except King LTD-S, use the suprasternal notch and the end of the airway into which the catheter will be placed as guides,
measure the depth desired for the catheter (judgment must be used regarding the depth of suctioning with cricothyrotomy and tracheostomy
tubes).
 
5.
 If applicable, remove ventilation devices from the airway.
 
6.
 With the thumb port of the catheter uncovered, insert the catheter through the airway device.
 
7.
 Once desired depth (measured in number 4 above) has been reached, occlude the thumb port and remove the suction catheter slowly.
 
8.
 Small volume (< 10 mL) of normal saline lavage may be used as needed.
 
9.
 Reattach ventilation device (e.g., bag-valve mask) and ventilate the patient.
 
10.
 Document time and result in the Patient Care Report (PCR).
