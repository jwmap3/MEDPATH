Recognize Sepsis
Elderly and very young.
Bed confined, immobile.
Recent surgery; invasive procedure.
Immunosuppressants/Chemotherapy.
Immunocompromised (HIV, transplant, diabetes, cancer).
Source of Infection
Illness, recent surgery/procedure.
Respiratory, GI, GU.
Recent antibiotics, steroids, immunosuppressants.
Vital Sign Warnings
Temp: > 38; < 36.
Pulse: > 90 bpm.
Blood pressure: <90 systolic or MAP < 65.
Resp. rate: > 20.
EtCO
 < 26.
Differential
Infection/sepsis.
Cancer/tumors/lymphomas.
Medication reaction.
Connective tissue disease.
Hyperthyroid.
Heat stroke.
Meningitis.
Pearls
Exam:
 SIRS = Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome = Fever > 38 (100.4) or < 36 (96.8); HR > 90 bpm; RR > 20; arterial PCO2 < 32.
Sepsis: one or more end organs begin to fail. Septic Shock: Sustained hypotension after aggressive fluid resuscitation.
Avoid hypoxia-->adequately oxygenate. Consider CPAP for moderate respiratory distress; intubate for altered mental status/respiratory
failure.
Avoid overventilation to prevent acute lung injury.
Avoid pressor therapy until adequate fluid resuscitation has been performed.
