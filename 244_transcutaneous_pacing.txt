PARAMEDIC
Clinical Indications:
Monitored heart rate less than 60 per minute with signs and symptoms of inadequate cerebral or cardiac perfusion such as:
High degree AV block.
Hypotension/Shock.
Pulmonary edema and/or cardiogenic shock.
Altered mental status associated with severe bradycardia, disorientation, confusion, etc.
Procedure
 
1.
 Attach standard four-lead monitor.
 
2.
 Apply defibrillation/pacing pads to chest and back:
One pad to left mid chest next to sternum; one pad to mid left posterior chest next to spine.
 
3.
 Activate pacing mode on monitor.
 
4.
 Adjust heart rate to 70 BPM for an adult and 100 BPM for a child.
 
5.
 Note pacer spikes on EKG screen.
 
6.
 Slowly increase output until capture of electrical rhythm on the monitor.
 
7.
 If unable to capture while at maximum current output, stop pacing immediately.
 
8.
 If capture observed on monitor, check for corresponding pulse and assess vital signs.
 
9.
 Consider the use of sedation or analgesia if patient is uncomfortable.
 
10.
 Document the dysrhythmia and the response to external pacing with ECG strips in the PCR.
