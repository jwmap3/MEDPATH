Pearls
Pain severity is a vital sign and must be recorded pre and post IV/IM/IN pain medications.
Vitals should be obtained pre, post, and at disposition with all pain medications.
Document drug allergies.
Observe for drug reaction.
Monitor pulse oximetry and ETCO2 when giving pain medications.
Use lower doses of narcotic pain medication initially in pediatrics and monitor for effect.
