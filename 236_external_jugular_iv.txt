AEMT
PARAMEDIC
Clinical Indications (Not for routine use)
Use in critical patients needing immediate medical intervention.
Must be ADULT patient.
   Contraindications
Must NOT have suspected C-spine injury.
Procedure
 
1. 
 Lie patient flat and turn head to side.
 
2.
 If patient is conscious, explain the procedure.
 
3.
 Prepare site with alcohol swab.
 
4.
 Have patient take deep breath and hold or bear down. This will distend the vein.
 
5.
 Perform venipuncture in line with vein. Once you get flash, advance a little more then slide catheter into place.
 
6.
 Have pt resume normal breathing pattern.
 
7.
 Attach saline lock and flush to assure patency.
 
8.
 Secure in place as you normally would.
 
9.
 Document in PCR.
