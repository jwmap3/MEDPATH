20 mg/kg

Maximum 1,000 mg (1 gram)
Contraindications
Patients with the risk of existing digitalis toxicity
Adverse Reactions
Rapid injection may cause tingling sensations, a calcium taste, or heat wave.
Peripheral vasodilatation, local burning, or moderate fall in BP.  
If infiltration occurs, IV administration at the site should be discontinued at once.
Precautions
Injections should be made slowly through a small needle into a large vein to minimize venous irritation and avoid undesirable reactions.
Medical Considerations
Irritating to veins and must not be injected into tissue, severe necrosis and sloughing may occur.
Mechanism of Actions
Hyperkalemia
Calcium channel blocker
