All Levels of Certification
1
. 
Competent patients maintain the right to refuse care and/or transport. If unsure, contact On-Line Medical Control.
2
. 
All patients refusing service will be:
a
. 
Informed of the availability of service and offered treatment and transport in a polite manner that is nonconfrontational.
b
. 
Advised to call 911 for emergency service if desired.
c
. 
Advised that they accept full responsibility for their actions.
3
. 
Contact Medical Control if ALS has been started and patient declines transport. Give the Medical Control Physician an explanation by recorded
device of the situation and request permission to discontinue ALS. The name of the physician who gave the order must be documented in the
PCR. When making contact with the physician, the contact should be done on a recorded device. Call 911 or use the hospital radio channel in
order to assure that the call is recorded. Calling the hospital direct will not record the call.
4
. 
Insulin dependent diabetics with hypoglycemic episodes may refuse transport after assessment of blood glucose and appropriate clinical
response to dextrose therapy is noted. Patient must have person at home to monitor and must be able to eat. Non-insulin diabetics on oral
hypoglycemic therapy with hypoglycemic episodes must be encouraged not to sign AMA and be evaluated at the hospital.
5
. 
Documentation:
a
. 
In the report narrative, describe the patient encounter, vital signs, advice given, that the patient is alert and orientated to person, place,
and time, and that the patient understands instructions given to him/her.
b
. 
If possible, have the patient sign the AMA form, have a third party witness the signature, and give a copy to the patient.
c
. 
Complete the "Patient Refusal of Care" procedure in the electronic call report.
6
. 
At no time will EMS professionals mention cost of transport, patient's insurance status, hospital billing or insurance practices, status of
system/unit availability, or any other non-clinical subject in an attempt to influence a patients decision to accept or decline transport.
