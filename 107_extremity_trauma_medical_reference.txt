In amputations, time is critical. Consider transport to Trauma center (see flowchart for more information).
Hip dislocation and knee and elbow fracture/dislocations have a high incidence of vascular compromise.
Urgently transport any injury with vascular compromise.
Blood loss may be concealed or not apparent with extremity injuries.
Severe bleeding not rapidly controlled may necessitate application of a tourniquet.
Lacerations must be evaluated for repair within 6 hours from the time of injury.
