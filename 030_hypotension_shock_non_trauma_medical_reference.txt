Pearls
Exam:
 mental status; skin; heart; lungs; abdomen; back; extremities; neuro.
Hypotension = SBP < 90 mmHg.
Consider orthostatic vital signs on non-trauma patients with suspected blood or fluid loss.
Consider all causes of shock and treat per protocol.
Vasopressors should be given through large bore antecubital site.
Use EXTREME caution when using vasopressors through peripheral line. Assure that line large bore, antecubital, flushes easily. If there is any
sign of infiltration, stop infusion and notify receiving facility.
