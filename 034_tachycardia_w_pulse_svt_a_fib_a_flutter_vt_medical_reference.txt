Pearls
Do not give AV nodal blockers to suspected pre-excited atrial fibrillation/aflutter (WPW). (I.e., diltiazem, beta blockers, digoxin.)
The Paramedic may maintain diltiazem infusions begun at referring facilities. Discontinue infusion if hypotension, bradycardia, allergic reaction
occurs. Notify On-Line Medical Control.
Document all effects of therapy/rhythm changes.
Maximum dose Diltiazem is 10 mg per dose.
