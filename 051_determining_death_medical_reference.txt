Differential
Attended death:
 First, death is attended when a person passes way in the presence of family and friends. Second, a death is legally attended in
situations that include:
Patient in a hospital or medical center.
A resident of long-term care or nursing facility.
An individual in hospice care.
An individual in regular contact with a primary care physician who has suffered from an extended illness.
The individual saw a primary care physician with a 30-day period prior to death.
Unattended death:
 A death in which the body is not found for days, weeks, or in some cases, months.
