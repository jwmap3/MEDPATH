Indications
1
. 
A single Paramedic crew or a non-Paramedic staffed ambulance.
Policy
1
. 
Generally, the highest care provider should attend the patient in the patient care area. An EMT may attend the patient if, and only if, the
Paramedic documents patient stability and is responsible to supervise.
a
. 
An ALS resource will be dispatched on every request for EMS service that is triaged by EMS/Communication Center as ALS. A first
responding, non-Paramedic ambulance cannot cancel the Paramedic resource if the patient they encounter does not meet the criteria for
non-Paramedic transport.
b
. 
The provider with the highest level of certification on scene shall conduct a detailed physical assessment and subjective interview with
the patient to determine their chief complaint and level of distress. If the Paramedic determines that the patient is stable and all patient
care needs can be managed by the EMT, patient care can be transferred to an EMT for transport to a hospital. All personnel are
encouraged to participate in patient care while on-scene; regardless of who "attends" with the patient while en route to the hospital. The
determination of who attends should be based upon the patient's immediate treatment needs and any reasonably anticipated treatment
needs while en route to the hospital. Both the transporting EMT and the Paramedic must sign a single PCR at the end of the run. The
PCR should contain a narrative that covers all aspects of assessment, care and disposition. 
c
. 
This should be done on one PCR.
2
. 
The following patients cannot be transferred to an EMT, have the Paramedic unit canceled, or be transported by a non-Paramedic ambulance
without requesting ALS intercept:
a
. 
Patients who have been medicated on the scene may only be transferred to an EMT whose formulary includes the medications that
were administered.
b
. 
Any patient suffering from chest pain of suspected cardiac origin, respiratory distress, hypertensive emergencies, multiple trauma, or
imminent childbirth.
c
. 
Any patient in which transport would be delayed by waiting for a unit with EMT certification to arrive.
d
. 
Any patient for which ALL EMS providers on scene do not agree can be safely transported without a Paramedic.
Certification Requirements
1
. 
Maintain knowledge of the indications, contraindications, technique, and possible complications of the procedure. Assessment of this
knowledge may be accomplished via quality assurance mechanisms, classroom demonstrations, skills stations, or other mechanisms as deemed
appropriate by the Toledo Fire & Rescue.
