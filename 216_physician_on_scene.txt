Policy
1
. 
The medical direction of prehospital care at the scene of an emergency is the responsibility of those most appropriately trained in providing
such care. All care should be provided within the rules and regulations of the State of Ohio and the State of Michigan.
Indications
1
. 
An Ohio licensed physician at the scene who wishes to assume medical responsibility for the patient for patients being treated in Ohio.
2
. 
A Michigan licensed physician at the scene who wishes to assume medical responsibility for the patient (i.e. a patient being treated in
Michigan).
3
. 
In Lucas County, certain EMS physicians are pre-designated as approved On-Scene Medical Control.
Procedure
1
. 
If a pre-existing "physician-patient" relationship does not exist, contact On-Line Medical Control for physician authorization; the Medical
Control physician will decide if the on-scene physician will be allowed to take control of patient care and issue medical orders.
2
. 
If a pre-existing "physician-patient" relationship does exist, the physician is authorized to take control of patient care and issue medical orders.
3
. 
Follow the orders of the authorized physician, even if they conflict with the existing local protocols, provided that they encompass skills and
medications approved by both the Toledo Fire & Rescue 
Department
 Medical Director and the State Medical Board.
4
. 
An intervening physician must accompany the patient to the hospital and take part in the documentation.
