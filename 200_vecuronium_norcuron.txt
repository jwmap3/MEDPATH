PARAMEDIC
Special Notes: 
Onset:
  1 - 3 minutes  
Half Life:
 60 - 120 minutes   
Peak Effect:
  4 - 6 minutes
Adult Indications & Dosing
Post ROSC Care
Vecuronium (Norcuron) 
10 mg IVP, IO
Pediatric Indications & Dosing
No pediatric doses for this medication.
Contraindications
Hypersensitivity to vecuronium or its components
Allergy to other neuromuscular blocking agents or aminosteroid compounds
Known or suspected myasthenia gravis or other neuromuscular disorders
Severe electrolyte imbalances
Severe hepatic disease
Severe renal impairment
Severe acid-base disturbances
Severe hypoxemia
Severe hypotension or shock
Severe burns or trauma Neuromuscular diseases associated with elevated serum potassium levels
Adverse Reactions
Cardiovascular System: Hypotension (low blood pressure), tachycardia (rapid heart rate)
Respiratory System: Respiratory depression, bronchospasm (constriction of airways)
Neuromuscular System: Muscle weakness, paralysis
Gastrointestinal System: Nausea, vomiting
General/Other: Allergic reactions, including rash or itching.
Precautions
Use with caution in patients with hepatic or renal disease or electrolyte abnormalities
Medical Considerations
Monitor for respiratory depression
Mechanism of Actions
Binds competitively to cholinergic receptors at the motor endplate, preventing the binding of acetylcholine
