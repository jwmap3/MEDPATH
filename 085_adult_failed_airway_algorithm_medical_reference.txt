Pearls
EMT may place supraglottic airway only in apneic and pulseless patients.
Continuous pulse oximetry should be used in all patients with inadequate respiratory function.
Continuous EtCO2 should be applied to all patients with respiratory failure and to all patients with advanced airway.
Notify OLMC as soon as possible about failed airway.
