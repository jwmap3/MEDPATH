Policy
Discontinuation of cardiopulmonary resuscitation and other advanced life saving interventions may be considered when ALL of the criteria below
have been met.
Procedure
1
. 
Adequate CPR has been administered.
2
. 
Endotracheal intubation and/or supraglottic airway ( SGA), ETT, or Cricothyrotomy placement has been successfully accomplished with
adequate ventilation (as per Airway protocol). If patient has a functioning tracheostomy in place, this is sufficient for advanced airway.
3
. 
IV/IO access has been achieved.
4
. 
Rhythm-appropriate medications and defibrillations for ventricular dysrhythmias have been administered according to protocol.
5
. 
In patients at least 18 years of age, failure to establish spontaneous and sustained circulation (palpable pulse) after 30 minutes of standard
resuscitation.  Consideration for expert consultation / OLMC regarding extension of resuscitative efforts, need for transport or termination of
efforts in the following situations: 
 
A. Witnessed Arrest; 
B. Bystander CPR; 
C. AED Shock PTA; 
D. Persistently elevated EtC0
 values; and 
E. Recurrent VF / Pulseless VT.
6
. 
Body temperature is at least 35 centigrade (95°F) for a patient who is submerged in cold water (water temperature less than  47°F (8.5°C).
7
. 
Health care providers on scene agree with decision to cease efforts.
8
. 
Contact On Line Medical Control for pronouncement and time of death.
Notes
1
. 
Family members and others present must be acknowledged and assisted.
2
. 
Disposition of the body as per the Deceased Persons Protocol.
