Pearls
Pain severity is a vital sign and must be recorded pre and post IV/IM pain medications.
Vitals should be obtained pre, post, and at disposition with all pain medications.
Contraindications to Morphine = hypotension, altered mental status, head injury, respiratory distress, severe COPD.
Document drug allergies.
When using ketamine, pt MUST be on monitor, with SpO2, ETCO2, have an IV and be given oxygen.
Nausea associate with pain medication administration, use Ondansetron (Zofran) per Nausea and Vomiting protocol
