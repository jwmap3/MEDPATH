Adverse Reactions
Reactions which may occur because of the solution or the technique of administration include febrile response, infection at the site of injection,
venous thrombosis or phlebitis extending from the site of injection, extravasation, and hypervolemia. If adverse reaction does occur, discontinue
infusion.
