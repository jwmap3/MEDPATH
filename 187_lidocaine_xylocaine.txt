Precautions
Contraindicated if allergic to other amide type anesthetics such as Nupercaine.
Caution in patients with greater than second degree heart block.
DC drug if signs of toxicity appear (i.e.: dizziness, convulsions or confusion.  
Convulsions may be the first sign of toxicity). Use in caution in patients with digitalis toxicity.
Medical Considerations
Observe closely for drug toxicity
Signs include: dizziness, confusion, delirium, seizures
