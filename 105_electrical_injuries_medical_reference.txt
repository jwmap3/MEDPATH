Ventricular fibrillation and asystole are most common dysrhythmias.
Damage often hidden - most severe damage to muscle, vessels, and nerves.
In multiple victim lightning incident, attend to victims in full arrest first. IF the victim did not arrest initially, it is likely they will survive. These
patients are often resuscitated with adequate CPR and ALS.
Do not overlook other trauma.
Lightning is a massive DC shock, most often leading to asystole as the dysrhythmia.
In lightning injuries, most of the current will travel over the body surface producing flash burns.
