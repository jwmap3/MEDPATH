History
Submersion in water regardless of depth.
Possible history of trauma.
Duration of immersion.
Temperature of water.
Fresh or salt water.
Signs and Symptoms
Unresponsive.
Mental status change.
Decreased or absent vital signs.
Vomiting.
Coughing.
Differential
Trauma.
Pre-existing medical condition.
Pressure injury (diving).
Incident barotrauma and decompression sickness.
Pearls
Exam:
 trauma survey; head; neck; chest; pelvis; back; extremities; skin; neuro.
With cold water there is no time limit – resuscitate all.
All victims should be transported for evaluation due to potential for worsening over next several hours.
All appropriately trained rescuers to remove victims from areas of danger.
With pressure injuries, consider transport to a hyperbaric chamber.
