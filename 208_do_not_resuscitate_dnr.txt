Policy
Any patient presenting to any component of Toledo Fire & Rescue 
Department
 with a completed Ohio Do Not Resuscitate (DNR) form shall have
the form honored and CPR and ALS therapy withheld in the event of cardiac arrest.
Purpose
1
. 
To honor the terminal wishes of the patient.
2
. 
To prevent the initiation of unwanted resuscitation.
Procedure
1
. 
When confronted with a patient or situation involving DNR, you must confirm the patient's status in order to honor the DNR form and
withhold CPR and ALS therapy using one of the following methods:
a
. 
A DNR Comfort Care card or form for the patient.
b
. 
A completed State of Ohio living will (declaration) form that states that the patient does not want CPR (in the case of a patient who has
been determined by two doctors to be in a terminal or permanently unconscious state).
c
. 
A DNR Comfort Care necklace or bracelet bearing the DNR Comfort Care official logo.
d
. 
A DNR order signed by the patient's attending physician or, when authorized by section 2133.211 of the Ohio Revised Code, a certified
nurse practitioner (CNP) or clinical nurse specialist (CNS).
e
. 
A verbal DNR order is issued by the patient's attending physician, CNP, or CNS. EMS personnel must verify the identify of the person
issuing the order. Examples of verification are:
Personal knowledge of the doctor/CNP/CNS.
List of practitioners with other identifying information such as addresses.
A return telephone call to verify information provided.
f
. 
Copies of the above items are sufficient.
2
. 
EMS workers are not required to search a person to see if they have DNR Identification but, if an EMS or other health care worker discovers
one of the qualifying items in the possession of a patient, the worker must make a reasonable effort to identify the patient in appropriate
circumstances. This can be done by:
a
. 
The patient or a family member, caregiver, or friend gives the patient's name.
b
. 
The health care worker knows the patient personally.
c
. 
Institutional identification band.
d
. 
Driver's license, passport, or other picture ID.

3
. 
A valid DNR form may be overridden by the request of:
a
. 
The patient.
b
. 
Health care power of attorney.
c
. 
An on-scene physician.
Notes
If you cannot verify the identify of a patient after reasonable efforts, still follow this protocol.
