Early aggressive treatment of pulmonary edema avoids intubation.
Avoid Nitro in patient who has used Viagra or Levitra in past 24 hours or Cialis in past 36 hours.
Consider myocardial infarction in all of these patients (cardiogenic shock).
Careful monitoring of LOC, BP, respiratory status with above interventions is essential.
Allow patient to remain in position of comfort to maximize breathing effort.
