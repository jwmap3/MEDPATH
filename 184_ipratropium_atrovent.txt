Precautions
May cause bronchoconstriction to worsen.  This is thought to be related to the hypotonicity of the solution or to additives, such as
benzalkonium chloride.  It is for this reason that beta-adrenergic agonists should be given first or in combination with ipratropium
bromide.
Use caution in patients with narrow angle glaucoma, prostatic hyperplasia or bladder-neck obstruction.
