PARAMEDIC
Clinical Indications:
Patients with a completely obstructed airway = "Can't intubate and can't ventilate situation."
Patients with severe facial trauma obscuring normal airway anatomy.
NOTE: This procedure is not recommended in children under age 12.
Procedure
 
1.
 Place patient in the supine position with the neck in a neutral position. Palpate the cricothyroid membrane between the thyroid and
cricoid membranes for orientation.
 
2.
 Prep the area.
 
3.
 Stabilize the thyroid cartilage with non-dominant hand.
 
4.
 Make a vertical incision with scalpel over the region of the cricothyroid membrane. Make a horizontal incision through the membrane.
Place finger in hole. Pass bougie over the finger into the trachea. Pass endotracheal tube/trach tube over bougie into the trachea. Inflate cuff.
 
5.
 Attach BVM to trach tube and attempt ventilation. Confirm placement with auscultation and capnography.
 
6.
 Secure tube and document patient response in PCR.
Complications
Creation of false passage.
Hemorrhage or hematoma formation.
Laceration of the esophagus.
