Precautions
Like all antiarrhythmic agents, may cause a worsening of existing arrhythmias or precipitate a new arrhythmia.
2% of patients were reported to have respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS).
May produce vasodilation and hypotension.
Do not use with irregular Tachyarrhythmias or Torsades.
