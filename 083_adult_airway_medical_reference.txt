Pearls
For this protocol, Adult is greater than or equal to 16 years of age.
Capnography used to assess adequacy of ventilation.
Oximetry MANDATORY to assess adequacy of oxygenation.
Capnometry is mandatory with all methods of intubation. Document results.
Continuous EtCO2 monitoring is MANDATORY for all advanced airway/intubated patients.
Do not assume hyperventilation is psychogenic – use oxygen.
ELM (external laryngeal manipulation) maneuver may be used to assist with difficult intubation's.
Place an SGA when unable to intubate a patient.
Utilize continuous pulse oximetry.
Place c collar to avoid extubation during transport and patient movement. For non-trauma patients, may remove on arrival to hospital.
Bougie should be used on any attempt, except in hyperangulated Video Laryngoscopy.
